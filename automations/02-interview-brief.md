# Routine 02 — Optional questions

Trigger: the author chooses material and requests help developing it. Read `AGENTS.md`
and `docs/pipeline.md` first. A scheduled check may offer optional reading or a question;
it must not select a subject for the author or create a publishing obligation.

Start with what seems interesting in the supplied material, the consequential gap, and
one useful question. Ask more only when needed or requested. A complete existing draft
needs no interview. Keeping the thought as a private fragment is a normal outcome.

Prefer concrete experience, what the author believes, and what remains uncertain before
suggesting counterframes. Research factual questions yourself. Never answer an
unresolved author judgment on their behalf. Personal details, humor, and scenes do not
need a business thesis. Do not require five questions, retelling, A/B calibration, a
prediction, or a title/last-line exercise.

If a brief is needed, keep the existing phone-compatible headings and numbered question
format. Only use public-safe material in this public repository; otherwise keep the
interaction in its verified private source. Do not promise that an authenticated UI
makes Git or PRs private.

```md
# Interview: <working title>

**Source:** <safe reference to author-selected material>
**Status:** Awaiting answers

## The idea in three sentences
<what seems interesting and what remains open; shorter is fine>

## Questions
1. <one question that materially helps this piece>

## Author answers
<author input only; English, 中文, or mixed; fragments welcome>
```

Optional `→ ` / `→ push: ` / `→ read: ` lines remain supported by the phone client.
Use them sparingly, only when helpful. They are model suggestions, not author answers.
Preserve `free` / `nudge-assisted` provenance on saved answers. Do not turn a suggested
answer into a Q-owned claim merely because it sounds plausible.

Only the author sets `Ready to draft`. Saving all answers, approaching expiry, or
answering a question does not authorize drafting, including a short Note. Leave unsigned
answers and their status intact. No deadline or substitute topic follows a skipped brief.
When the author marks supplied material ready, scope still follows that material, and
publication remains a separate explicit decision after bilingual preview.

If an authorized notification is useful: link the chosen piece and ask the one question.
Do not add a weekly time commitment, deadline, or repeated reminder for paused work.
