# Routine 05 — Optional maintenance

Trigger: an optional maintenance review. Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/pipeline.md`, and
`docs/editorial-critic.md`. Preserve existing author input and confirmed preferences.

- Check broken public links, bilingual lifecycle consistency, and stale review results.
  Fix safe mechanical errors; substantive scope changes require a new critic pass.
- Review actual feedback when useful: did a draft add unsupported theory, invent a
  position, or need a late author rescue? Also check that supported drafts passed
  without gratuitous cuts. Critic verdict counts and word counts are not quality scores.
- An old prediction or relevant new event may prompt an optional reading suggestion.
  Archive content is historical context, not current Q-explicit adoption or drafting
  intent. Never manufacture a follow-up to fill the calendar.
- Do not impose output, Essay, origination, or draft-to-publish targets. No-output months,
  short personal pieces, unanswered questions, and intentionally private fragments are
  normal. Do not downgrade or close drafts, expire author input, or delete voice
  proposals because a clock elapsed.
- Honor explicit adopted positions and confirmed voice rules. Automated voice mining,
  monthly retelling, per-draft calibration, and automatic promotion of a single edit
  into a permanent rule are deferred. Optional notes about a kept/rejected passage must
  use the verified private boundary; do not archive raw examples in public Git.
- Propose lifecycle changes only from explicit author/editorial instructions; preserve
  paired notices and stable links. Similarity does not establish that Q changed views.

Report only concrete findings or a useful action. Do not commit empty retrospectives,
celebrate streaks, or create a publishing obligation. A short run response can say that
nothing needed attention. Notify through an authorized channel only when useful.
