## The idea in three sentences

"Taste is the new moat in the AI era" has hardened into a cliché precisely because it stays vague and creative-facing — everyone repeats it, almost nobody says what taste *is* in a given job. The sharper claim you gestured at: taste is just the creative world's name for domain judgment — knowing what is good enough and appropriate in a specific context — and it is the same scarce skill a consultant, a lawyer, or an operator has always sold, now suddenly visible because agents made the production around it cheap. The strongest counterargument is that stretching "taste" to cover consulting judgment or engineering discipline empties the word until it means nothing, and taste really is a narrower, aesthetic, consumer-facing thing.
