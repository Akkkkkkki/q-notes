## The one finding that matters more than the numbers

`docs/pipeline.md` §4.4 is explicit: "If there is no critic result... the gate cannot say
Ready." On 2026-08-21, the ship gate posted that exact sentence on both `#64` and `#104`,
naming the missing `## Editorial critic` comment as the sole blocker on each. Three days
later, both merged anyway:

- **`#64`** — the 08-21 gate comment says "Blocked — no editorial-critic pass exists on
  this PR... Routine 03b needs to run against `105e3109` before this gate can post Ready
  again." On 08-24 the author sent a `Downgrade to note` Desk comment, a trim was pushed,
  and the very next comment is "**Ready to ship** — applied the downgrade request." No
  critic comment appears anywhere in the PR's history, and the merge went through minutes
  later.
- **`#104`/`#125`** — the same 08-21 gate comment, the same blocker, the same pattern: a
  `Downgrade to note` Desk comment on 08-24, a trim, then "Superseded by #125... has now
  merged." `#125`'s entire comment thread is a Codex bot review and nothing else — no
  critic pass, on either PR, at any point.

This is not the "stale `KEEP` carried past a semantic change" failure §9 already tracks —
there was never a `KEEP` to go stale. It's a plainer gap: the gate can *say* "blocked,"
but nothing stops a merge from happening anyway once the author acts on a Desk comment,
because Routine 03b is a prompt-based review with no mechanical enforcement behind it (the
hard gate in `docs/pipeline.md` §8 is CI — tier/parity/build — which none of this touches).
Both pieces are fine on their merits: the `#64` and `#104` trims are exactly the kind of
subtractive, single-idea-only cuts a `KEEP` would very plausibly have produced. But "would
plausibly have passed anyway" is not the same thing as actually running the check the
process requires before every ship, and the fact that the author had to independently spot
both over-completions by hand, on the two PRs the critic skipped, is the sharper version
of the same gap. **Concrete fix worth considering next: have Routine 04 refuse to post any
"Ready to ship" language at all — including after a Desk downgrade is applied — on a PR
with zero critic comments, rather than only checking for a stale one.**

Smaller version of the same shape: for the second month running, a shipped piece's backlog
bookkeeping wasn't updated at merge time and had to be corrected retroactively —
`ai-native-game-is-a-test` and `wallet-is-not-a-conscience` both carry a note to that
effect in `research/backlog.md`, exactly like `taste-is-a-bet` did in July. Two consecutive
months of the same slip suggests the drafter/gate doesn't reliably touch backlog status at
merge time, not that each instance is a one-off.

On the good-news side: the scheduler gap July's retro flagged as the real root cause of
that month's misses looks resolved. Scout, interviewer, drafter, and ship-gate routines
all ran repeatedly and roughly on their weekly cadence throughout August (five scout runs,
four interview briefs, six-plus drafter-related PRs, weekly Friday gate passes) — this
month's finding is a gap in what the loop enforces, not whether it runs at all.
