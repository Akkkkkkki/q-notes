Yesterday I tried to find a note about a broken export. I remembered the problem, the afternoon, and the cup of tea beside my keyboard. I couldn't remember the title. Apparently “Some thoughts” had seemed useful when I saved it.

I opened four files before I found the right one. The note itself was fine: three lines describing the failure and a screenshot of the button. I hadn't needed a longer explanation. I had needed a name I could recognize a week later.

I changed the title to “Export button drops the final row.” Then I tried searching for “export” and found it immediately. That tells me something about this one search, with this one note. It doesn't tell me how anyone else should organize a notebook.

I'm leaving the rest alone for now. There are probably other vague titles in the folder, but fixing every one would turn a small annoyance into an afternoon project. Next time I save a note, I'll try naming the thing that happened. If that helps me find it later, I'll keep doing it. The screenshot can stay exactly where it is. It already did its job; the title was the part I had to remember.
